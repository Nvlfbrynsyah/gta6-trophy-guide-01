THE ROUTE

Roadmap to the platinum

Four stages · save early, sweep once
  1. 01

    Story playthrough

    Play the Prologue (Gone Fishing), Chapters 1–4, then the final mission. Make a separate manual save at each chapter start and before the finale: there is no mission replay.

  2. 02

    Side content

    Complete 8 stranger strands and sample every activity. Win 20 different races, visit every Top Fun venue, and finish fishing, arcade, and partner activities.

  3. 03

    Collectibles & grind

    Clear 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicle discoveries, 250 stunts, 50 Challenges, and 5 Bank Vaults. Register 10 vehicles, launder $250,000, spend $100,000, buy all garages, and gold the range.

  4. 04

    Cleanup + platinum

    Explore the remaining map, resolve one-off encounters, and check every locked trophy. Reach 100% completion, verify meditation against your early save, and earn Vice Got a Price! for the platinum.
